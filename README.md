# End Village

A Minecraft Forge 1.20.1 mod. Adds villages to the End, built on their own floating islands in the End Highlands and Midlands. The villagers are **Shulker Villagers**: peaceful shulkers whose shell color shows their job. Right-click one to trade.

| Job | Shell | Trades |
|---|---|---|
| Chorus Farmer | lime | buys chorus fruit, sells chorus flowers and end rods |
| Purpur Mason | light gray | buys end stone, sells purpur and end stone bricks |
| Void Librarian | white | buys paper, sells enchanted books and xp bottles |
| Shell Keeper | black | buys iron and diamonds, sells shulker shells and boxes |
| Star Cartographer | light blue | sells maps, compasses and an End City map |
| Dragon Cleric | yellow | buys and sells ender pearls, sells dragon's breath and eyes of ender |

Trades restock every half day. `tools/make_village.py` builds the village structure files.
